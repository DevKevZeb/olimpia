📋 Step-by-Step Test

1 Open Registration & Fill Personal Data

Action: Open the registration form and fill in personal information (CI, name, email, phone, select a grade)

Expected: Should see in console:

❌ No ejecutando fetchAreasPorGrado porque no estamos en step 2

Should NOT see: API calls to areas endpoint or area loading messages

2 Navigate to Step 2 (Areas Selection)

Action: Click "Siguiente" button to go to step 2

Expected: Should see in console:

🔄 useAreasSelection: useEffect disparado - values: {isStep2: true} ✅ Condiciones cumplidas, haciendo llamada API... 📊 Datos recibidos de la API: {...} ✅ Áreas cargadas exitosamente: X áreas

Expected in UI: Areas should be visible and selectable

3 Test Grade Change

Action: Go back to step 1, change grade, return to step 2

Expected: Areas should reload for the new grade without errors
